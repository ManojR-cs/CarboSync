📐 Methodology & Factor Provenance

Core Calculation Formulas

VKT_k (km/year) = N_k × D_k × 2 × Y
Emissions_k (kg CO₂/year) = VKT_k × EF_k
Total Baseline (t CO₂/year) = Σ Emissions_k ÷ 1000

Where N_k = daily vehicles, D_k = one-way distance (km), Y = academic days, EF_k = emission factor (kg CO₂/veh-km)

Scope & Boundary Disclosure

🔬
Included: Tailpipe CO₂ from commuting by Cars, Motorcycles & Scooters, Buses, and Other fuel-burning vehicles.
Excluded: EVs (zero tailpipe), Walking, Cycling, Lifecycle/upstream manufacturing emissions. [Assumption]

Anti-Double-Counting Rules

  • Each vehicle is counted once per one-way trip.
  • Round-trip factor (×2) is applied in VKT formula — not in raw counts.
  • Bicycles detected in CV are counted for observation but excluded from all emissions calculations.
  • Shuttle Scenario 3 explicitly separates avoided private emissions from added shuttle diesel emissions.
